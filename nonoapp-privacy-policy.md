# NoNoApp Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026 (version 1.5.1)

NoNoApp helps you use selected apps and websites less. Before one of them opens, NoNoApp asks you to do something first: answer a question, wait, make noise, walk, or ask a trusted person.

**In short:**

- Your NoNo list, your answers, your statistics and the websites you visit stay on your phone.
- NoNoApp does **not** collect usage statistics.
- If NoNoApp crashes, it sends a **technical crash report** so the bug can be fixed. You can turn this off.
- No account, no ads, no advertising ID. Nothing is sold.

## What NoNoApp stores on your phone

NoNoApp saves the following information, only on your device:

- **Your NoNo list.** The apps and websites you picked, and any removal you have scheduled.
- **Your mode.** The unlock mode you use, plus any mode change you have started.
- **App usage time.** How long you used your apps today and this week. Android provides this through Usage Access.
- **Open attempts and unlocks.** When you tried to open a NoNo app or website, when you unlocked one, and for how long. Recent unlocks make the next unlock harder.
- **Your answers.** In Reflect mode, the text you type. Your recent answers are kept so you can't reuse the same answer.
- **Session results.** How long you planned to use an app, whether you got what you wanted, and how you felt afterward.
- **Bookings.** In Patience mode, when your booked time becomes available.
- **Trusted person.** In Trusted person mode, their name, their phone number (if you send requests by WhatsApp or SMS) and a secret key shared only between your NoNoApp and theirs.
- **People you approve for.** If you are someone's trusted person, their name and the shared secret key.
- **Timing records.** NoNoApp saves time checkpoints so changing the phone's clock can't skip a wait.

NoNoApp keeps at most the last 1,000 session results and the last 40 answers. Older entries are deleted automatically.

## Crash reports

If NoNoApp crashes, it automatically sends a crash report to **Firebase Crashlytics** (Google), so the developer learns about the bug and can fix it. You can turn this off at any time under **Send crash reports** (during setup or in the Mode tab); unsent reports are then deleted.

**What a crash report contains:** the technical error (stack trace), the app version, device model, Android version, free memory and storage, whether the phone was rotated or in the background, the time of the crash, a random installation ID, and the NoNoApp mode you use (for example "Patience").

**What it never contains:** app names, website addresses, your answers, your trusted person's name or number, or your advertising ID.

Google processes this data on behalf of the developer, as described in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Firebase data processing terms](https://firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms). The data may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

The legal basis is the developer's legitimate interest in finding and fixing errors (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time by turning the setting off.

## Trusted person mode

In this mode, every unlock needs a code from a person you trust. That person also uses NoNoApp (it needs no permissions on their phone). There is no server and no website in between.

- **Connecting:** your NoNoApp creates an invitation that you send through WhatsApp, your SMS app or another app you choose. Your trusted person pastes it into their NoNoApp and sends back an answer. Both apps then share a secret key. The key itself is never part of a message.
- **Asking for time:** the message you send contains your name for the app or website (for example "YouTube"), the time you ask for, and a random request number. Your trusted person's NoNoApp shows the request and, if they approve, a 6-digit code that they send back to you.
- All of this stays on the two phones. NoNoApp does not send these messages itself; **you** send them with your messaging app, which handles them under its own privacy policy.

## What NoNoApp does not do

- It does not sell or share your data for advertising.
- It does not contain advertising or social tracking tools.
- It does not need an account.

## Permissions

NoNoApp only asks for permissions it needs to work.

| Permission | What it is used for |
| --- | --- |
| Accessibility service | Notices which app is open, so the unlock screen can appear before a NoNo app. In web browsers it also reads the **address bar** (for example `instagram.com`), so websites on your NoNo list can be blocked. NoNoApp reads **nothing else on the screen**: no messages, passwords, text fields or page content. Addresses of websites that are not on your list are not stored, and none of this information is ever sent anywhere. |
| Usage access | Shows how long you use each app, and backs up the detection of the open app. |
| Display over other apps | Shows the timer and the end-of-session screen on top of an unlocked app. |
| Microphone (Loud mode only) | Measures how loud it is while the loudness meter is on screen. Only the volume level is calculated. **Nothing is recorded, saved or sent.** The microphone is used only while you are on the loudness screen. |
| Physical activity (Walk mode only) | Counts your steps while the walking screen is open. Steps are not saved after the unlock. |
| Internet | Only used to send crash reports. |

NoNoApp asks for the microphone or physical activity permission only if you choose the mode that needs it. You can take back any permission in Android settings at any time. Some features will then stop working.

## Backups

Android's cloud backup is turned off for NoNoApp, so your answers and statistics are not copied to your Google account.

## Deleting your data

To delete all NoNoApp data on your phone, either:

- uninstall NoNoApp, or
- go to *Android Settings → Apps → NoNoApp → Storage* and tap *Clear storage*.

Crash reports that were already sent are deleted automatically after 90 days. To have them deleted earlier, email the address below. Because the reports don't contain your name, include roughly when NoNoApp crashed and on which device model. To have them deleted earlier, email the address below. Because the data is anonymous, include roughly when you used NoNoApp and on which device model.
